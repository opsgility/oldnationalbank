# Pre-Work — Before the Copilot for Legal Session

**Do this the day before the session. It takes about 10 minutes.**

The session is hands-on. Every exercise uses real Old National Bank sample documents, and Copilot in Word can only reason over files it can reach in your Microsoft 365 environment. To follow along, you must have the sample documents saved in **your OneDrive** (or a shared SharePoint location the instructor provides) before we start.

---

## Why OneDrive matters

Copilot in Word works best when your document is **saved in OneDrive or SharePoint**, not only on your local C: drive. Cloud-saved files are what make these features work:

- **Reference other files in a prompt** — Copilot can pull from documents stored in your OneDrive/SharePoint.
- **Version history** — you can compare and restore earlier versions of a contract (used in Exercise 2 and 3).
- **Real-time collaboration** — comments and @mentions route to colleagues.

If a file lives only on your desktop, these capabilities are limited or unavailable.

---

## Step 1 — Confirm you have access

Before uploading, confirm the following. If any is missing, contact your IT/Copilot administrator **before** the session.

- [ ] You can sign in to Microsoft 365 (Office.com) with your ONB account.
- [ ] You have a **Microsoft 365 Copilot** license assigned (open Word → look for the **Copilot** button on the Home ribbon).
- [ ] You can open **OneDrive** from Office.com and see your files.
- [ ] You can open a document in **Word for the web** (our classroom baseline).

---

## Step 2 — Create a folder in OneDrive

1. Go to **office.com** and sign in.
2. Open **OneDrive**.
3. Click **+ Add new → Folder**.
4. Name it: **Copilot Legal Session**.
5. Download from the assets folder - https://github.com/opsgility/oldnationalbank/tree/main/assets

## Step 4 — Quick readiness check (1 minute)

1. In OneDrive, open the **Consulting Agreement** in **Word for the web**.
2. Click the **Copilot** button (Home ribbon or the side pane).
3. Type: `Summarize this agreement in 3 sentences.`
4. If you get a summary back, you are ready. If the Copilot button is missing or greyed out, contact your administrator before the session.
5. Continue through every Document uploaded to cache each file in your MRU (Most Recently Used)..
