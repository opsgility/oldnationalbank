ILLINOIS
- Mandated reporter for financial institutions: No general mandate on banks specifically; reporting is permissive/encouraged. Mandatory reporters are defined occupational classes (e.g., certain licensed professionals).
- Reporting agency: Adult Protective Services (APS) for community-based adults; long-term care goes to a separate channel. Law enforcement for crimes in progress.
- Trigger/threshold: Reasonable suspicion of financial exploitation of an eligible adult (60+ or 18–59 with disability).
- Timeframe: Promptly upon suspicion; no fixed statutory hour-count for banks (permissive).
- Information-sharing exception: Safe harbor permits disclosure to APS/law enforcement; immunity for good-faith reports. [verify cite — IL APS Act]

INDIANA
- Mandated reporter: Broad "any individual who believes or has reason to believe" an endangered adult is a victim must report. Banks/employees fall within "any individual."
- Reporting agency: APS (county) and/or law enforcement.
- Trigger: Belief or reason to believe endangered adult is being exploited/neglected.
- Timeframe: Immediately/promptly upon belief.
- Information-sharing exception: Good-faith immunity; permitted disclosure to APS and law enforcement. [verify cite — IN endangered adult statute]

CALIFORNIA
- Mandated reporter: Yes for "mandated reporters of financial abuse" — includes officers/employees of financial institutions.
- Reporting agency: APS and/or local law enforcement; financial-abuse reports may route to APS.
- Trigger: Knowledge or reasonable suspicion of financial abuse of an elder (65+) or dependent adult.
- Timeframe: Report as soon as practicable; written follow-up within set days. [verify exact days]
- Information-sharing exception: Permitted disclosure; immunity for mandated reporters acting in good faith. [verify cite — CA W&I Code elder abuse]

FLORIDA
- Mandated reporter: Yes — "any person" who knows or suspects abuse/exploitation of a vulnerable adult must report.
- Reporting agency: Central Abuse Hotline (DCF).
- Trigger: Knowledge or reasonable suspicion of exploitation of a vulnerable adult.
- Timeframe: Immediately via hotline.
- Information-sharing exception: Immunity for good-faith reports; confidentiality of reporter identity. [verify cite — FL Adult Protective Services Act]

NEW YORK
- Mandated reporter: No general "any person" mandate; reporting is largely permissive for banks. Specific programs may apply.
- Reporting agency: Adult Protective Services (local DSS); law enforcement for crimes.
- Trigger: Reasonable cause to suspect financial exploitation of an at-risk adult.
- Timeframe: Promptly (permissive).
- Information-sharing exception: Disclosure permitted to APS/law enforcement; check Banking Law provisions on delaying disbursements. [verify cite]
